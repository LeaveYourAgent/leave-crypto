# Security

Leave's security design is described in plain words at https://leaveyouragent.com/security, and in technical detail at https://github.com/LeaveYourAgent/leave-security.

To report a vulnerability, email chris@leaveyouragent.com (also listed at https://leaveyouragent.com/.well-known/security.txt). The reporting policy is at https://leaveyouragent.com/security#report.

Please do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.
